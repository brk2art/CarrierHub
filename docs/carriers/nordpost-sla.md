# NordPost service terms for merchants

NordPost is a fictional postal carrier used as test data in CarrierHub. These terms apply to parcels a merchant books
with NordPost through CarrierHub. Amounts are in Danish kroner (DKK).

## Promised delivery time

Every NordPost parcel has a promised delivery time, set when the parcel is booked. Delays caused by Danish public
holidays do not count: the promised time moves by one working day for each public holiday between booking and
delivery.

## Late delivery compensation

NordPost compensates a late parcel only when it is delivered more than 24 hours after the promised time. Then
NordPost refunds 25% of the shipping fee. A parcel delivered up to 24 hours late gets no compensation.

A merchant can file a late delivery claim before the parcel arrives, but NordPost decides it only after delivery, or
after the parcel counts as lost.

A late delivery claim must be filed within 30 days after the promised time.

## Failed delivery attempts

If NordPost attempts delivery before the promised time and the recipient is not at home, the parcel is not late. The
parcel then waits at the nearest NordPost service point for 7 days.

## Lost parcels

A parcel counts as lost when it has not been delivered 10 working days after the promised time. NordPost pays the value
of the goods, up to DKK 2,500 per parcel, or up to the insured value if the merchant bought NordPost Insurance.

A lost parcel claim must be filed within 30 days after the parcel counts as lost.

## Damaged parcels

Damage must be reported within 7 days after delivery, with photos of the damage. The merchant must keep the packaging
until NordPost has decided the claim, because NordPost may ask to inspect it. NordPost pays the value of the goods, up
to DKK 2,500 per parcel.

## Filing a claim

Claims are filed through CarrierHub, one claim per parcel and reason. NordPost answers within 15 working days.
