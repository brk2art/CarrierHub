# FastShip service terms for merchants

FastShip is a fictional express carrier used as test data in CarrierHub. These terms apply to parcels a merchant books
with FastShip through CarrierHub. Amounts are in Danish kroner (DKK).

## Promised delivery time

Every FastShip parcel has a promised delivery time, set when the parcel is booked. A parcel is delivered on time when
it is delivered at or before the promised time.

## Late delivery compensation

If a parcel is delivered more than 2 hours after the promised time, FastShip refunds 50% of the shipping fee. If it is
delivered more than 24 hours after the promised time, FastShip refunds the whole shipping fee. A parcel delivered up to
2 hours late gets no compensation.

A late delivery claim must be filed within 14 days after delivery.

## Failed delivery attempts

If FastShip attempts delivery before the promised time and the recipient is not at home, the parcel is not late, even
if it is delivered later. The promised time is met by the first attempt. A failed attempt made after the promised time
does not stop the parcel from being late.

## Lost parcels

A parcel counts as lost when it has not been delivered 5 days after the promised time. FastShip pays the value of the
goods, up to DKK 5,000 per parcel. The merchant must show the value with an invoice or receipt.

A lost parcel claim must be filed within 30 days after the promised time.

## Damaged parcels

Damage must be reported within 3 days after delivery, with photos of the parcel, the packaging and the damaged goods.
FastShip pays the repair cost or the value of the goods, up to DKK 5,000 per parcel. Damage reported later than 3 days
after delivery is not compensated.

## What is not compensated

FastShip does not compensate delays caused by severe weather warnings issued by the Danish Meteorological Institute, or
by customs checks. Goods that are not packed in a closed outer box are not covered for damage.

## Filing a claim

Claims are filed through CarrierHub, one claim per parcel and reason. FastShip answers within 10 working days.
