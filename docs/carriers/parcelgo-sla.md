# ParcelGo service terms for merchants

ParcelGo is a fictional parcel carrier used as test data in CarrierHub. These terms apply to parcels a merchant books
with ParcelGo through CarrierHub. Amounts are in Danish kroner (DKK).

## Promised delivery time

Every ParcelGo parcel has a promised delivery time, set when the parcel is booked. A parcel is delivered on time when
it is delivered at or before the promised time.

## Late delivery compensation

For any parcel delivered after the promised time, however late, ParcelGo gives the merchant a voucher of DKK 25 for
future shipments. ParcelGo does not refund the shipping fee for late parcels.

A late delivery claim must be filed within 7 days after delivery.

## Failed delivery attempts

If ParcelGo attempts delivery before the promised time and the recipient is not at home, the parcel is not late.
ParcelGo tries once more on the next working day.

## Lost parcels

A parcel counts as lost when it has not been delivered 7 days after the promised time. ParcelGo pays the value of the
goods, up to DKK 3,000 per parcel.

A lost parcel claim must be filed within 30 days after the promised time.

## Damaged parcels

Damage must be reported within 48 hours after delivery, with photos of the parcel and the damaged goods. Damage
reported later is not compensated. ParcelGo pays the value of the goods, up to DKK 3,000 per parcel.

## Filing a claim

Claims are filed through CarrierHub, one claim per parcel and reason. ParcelGo answers within 5 working days.
